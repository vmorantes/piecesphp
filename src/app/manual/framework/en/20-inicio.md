# Home

The home screen is the first thing you see when you log in: a summary of what concerns you at a glance.

## The home screen
<!-- ve: admin -->

How to get there: **“Home”**, in the side menu.

![The home screen](img/admin.webp)

At the top you see a greeting with your name. Below there are cards, each with its topic and a link to the details:

- **My sessions**: how many sessions you have open with your account, with the link **“See my sessions”**.
- **Users and sign-ins**: active users and today's sign-ins, with the link **“See the sign-ins”**.
- **Errors and notices**: it is collapsed. Click **“Expand”** to see the figures. Its link is **“See the errors”**.
- **General report**: a summary of the platform's figures, with the link **“View the report”**.

## General report
<!-- ve: reports-manage-admin-generic-report-view -->

The platform's figures: publications, organizations and users.

![General report](img/reports-manage-admin-generic-report-view.webp)

How to get there: from the **“General report”** card on the home screen, with **“View the report”**.

What you see:

- The **available AI tokens**.
- The **publications to validate**, with the link **“See the approvals”**.
- Two donut charts: **“Total of general users”** and **“Total Organizations”**. Each one splits the total by country and
  shows the number for each part.

This screen only shows figures: it has no forms.

## About
<!-- ve: admin-about-framework -->

Platform data: the installed version, its date, a description, the own libraries, and the third-party plugins and
dependencies with their license.

![About](img/admin-about-framework.webp)

How to get there: your user menu, at the top right › **“About”**.

What you see:

- The **installed version**, the **date** and the **commit** of that version.
- The **“GitHub”** button, which opens the PiecesPHP repository, the framework the platform is built on.
- The sections **“Description”**, **“Own libraries”** and **“Plugins, scripts, and dependencies”**.
