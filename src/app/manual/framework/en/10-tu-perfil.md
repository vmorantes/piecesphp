# Your profile

Everything about your account is in your name menu, at the top right.

## Your details

Choose **“Edit account”**: your account opens in a side panel. Change what you need and save.

## Your password

Choose **“Change password”**. To change it, the system first asks for your current password.

## Your account security
<!-- ve: my-space-admin-user-security -->

In your user menu, choose **“Security”**: next to it you see whether your two-step verification is **“Active”** or
**“Inactive”**. The **“Security”** screen opens on the two-step verification tab. On the **“My sessions”** tab you see
the sessions open with your account. For each one you can **“Close”** the one you do not recognize or **“Extend”** your
own, and **“Close the other sessions”** closes all of them except the one you are using.
