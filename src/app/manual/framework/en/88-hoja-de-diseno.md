# Design sheet

This is the page where you see every component of the panel (buttons, tabs, colors and so on) with its example.

![Design sheet](img/admin-design-sheet.webp)

To get there, use the gear button in the top bar: **“System settings” › “System” › “Design sheet”**. The screen's breadcrumbs
are **Home › Design sheet**.
