# My profile

What others see about you in the Directory and on the map. It only appears if your account type has a profile.

## How to get there

Choose **“My profile”** in your name menu, at the top right. At the top, the breadcrumbs read **Home › My profile**.

![How to get there](img/my-profile-admin-my-profile.webp)

## What you see

The form has two tabs: **“Personal data”** and **“Professional data”**. Required fields include **Position**,
**Nationality** and **Country**. There is also a map where you mark your location.

## What you can do

- **“Account”**: goes back to your account details.
- **“View my profile”**: opens your profile card as others see it.
- **“Save”** or **“Cancel”**.
