# Subscribers

The list of people who have subscribed.

## The subscribers list
<!-- ve: newsletter-admin-list -->

How to get there: **“Subscribers”**, in the side menu.

![The subscribers list](img/newsletter-admin-list.webp)

The table shows: **#**, **Name**, **Email**, **Accepts receiving emails** and **Actions**. In each row you can **“Edit”** or **“Delete”**.

At the top are **“Add subscriber”** and **“Export current list”**.

## Add and edit a subscriber
<!-- ve: newsletter-admin-forms-add, newsletter-admin-forms-edit -->

How to get there: click **“Add subscriber”**, or **“Edit”** in a subscriber's row.

![Add and edit a subscriber](img/newsletter-admin-forms-add.webp)

| Field | Required | What it is for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Name | Yes | The subscriber's name. |
| Email | Yes | Their email address. |
| Accepts receiving emails | No | Tick it if the person agrees to receive emails. |

Buttons: **“Save”** and, when editing, **“Delete”**.
