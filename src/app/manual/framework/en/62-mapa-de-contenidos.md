# Content map

It is a map for viewing content by its location.

## How to get there

From the side menu: **“Content map”**.

![How to get there](img/geo-admin-viewer.webp)

## What you can do

- Zoom the map in and out with the zoom buttons.
- Switch the background between **“Streets”** and **“Satellite”**.
- Open **“Filters”** to narrow what is shown, and **“Clear the filters”** to see everything again.
- Open the **“Legend”** to understand the symbols. The filters and legend panels are closed with **“Close”**.
