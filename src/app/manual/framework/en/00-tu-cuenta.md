# Your account

This manual explains the screens you can use with your account. You only see the chapters for what you are allowed to
do: if your account type changes, the manual changes with it. The same applies inside each screen: if an action named in
the manual does not appear for you, your user type does not have it.

## Logging in and out

1. On the login screen, choose how to log in:
    - **“Password”**: type your username or email and your password, then click **“Log in”**.
    - **“Access code”**: type your username or email and click **“Send code”**. A six-digit code arrives by email; type
      it (or paste it whole) into the six boxes and click **“Verify and log in”**. The code expires after a few minutes
      (the screen says how many); if it did not arrive, wait for **“Resend code”** to become active.
2. If your account has two-step verification, it then asks for the code from your app.
3. If something fails, the message appears inside the form and the field with the problem is highlighted: fix it and
   try again.
4. To log out, open your name menu, at the top right, and choose **“Logout”**.

![Logging in and out](img/users-form-login.webp)

!!! tip "If you cannot log in"
    On the login screen, the help link lets you recover your password or username, or report another problem.

## Text size and color mode

- **Color mode**: open the menu under your name, at the top right, and choose under **“Color mode”**: **“Light”**,
  **“Dark”** or **“System”** (your device's).
- **Text size**: in that same menu, **“Preferences”** takes you to the **“Preferences”** tab of **“My account”**.
  Under **“Text size”**, choose **“Normal”**, **“Large”** or **“Larger”**.

Both apply at once and are saved in your account. Until you choose, you see whatever the site's administrator chose.

## Help

The question mark icon in the top bar opens the **help center**: this manual and, if your organization has it
configured, the form to **contact support**.
