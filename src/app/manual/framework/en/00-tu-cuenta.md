# Your account

This manual explains the screens you can use with your account. You only see the chapters for what you are allowed to
do: if your account type changes, the manual changes with it. The same applies inside each screen: if an action named in
the manual does not appear for you, your user type does not have it.

## Logging in and out

1. On the login screen, choose how to log in:
    - **“Password”**: type your username or email and your password, then click **“Log in”**.
    - **“Access code”**: type your username or email and click **“Send code”**. A six-digit code arrives by email; type
      it (or paste it whole) into the six boxes and click **“Verify and log in”**. The code expires after a few minutes
      (the screen says how many); if it did not arrive, wait for **“Resend code”** to become active.
2. If your account has two-step verification, it then asks for the code from your app.
3. If something fails, the message appears inside the form and the field with the problem is highlighted: fix it and
   try again.
4. To log out, open your name menu, at the top right, and choose **“Logout”**.

![Logging in and out](img/users-form-login.webp)

!!! tip "If you cannot log in"
    On the login screen, the help link lets you recover your password or username, or report another problem.

## Your preferences

In the menu under your name, at the top right, **“Preferences”** takes you to the **“Preferences”** tab of **“My
account”**: how you see the panel. It has two cards and, below them, a **“Preview”** with sample text, buttons and
labels that change at once with what you choose:

- **Appearance**:
  - **Color mode**: **“Light”**, **“Dark”** or **“System”** (your device's). You also have it in the menu under your
    name, under **“Color mode”**, to change it quickly.
  - **Text size**: **“Normal”**, **“Large”** or **“Larger”**.
  - **Text spacing**: **“Normal”** or **“Wide”**, with more space between lines, paragraphs, words and letters.
- **Reading and motion**:
  - **Contrast**: **“Automatic”** (it follows your device) or **“More contrast”**, with stronger text and colors.
  - **Motion**: **“Automatic”** (it follows your device) or **“Reduce motion”**, with fewer animations.
  - **Transparency**: **“Like the system”** (it follows your device, if reducing transparency is turned on there) or
    **“Reduce”**, with solid backgrounds instead of translucent ones.
  - **Focus indicator**: **“Normal”** or **“Strong”**, with a more visible outline on what you have selected when you
    move with the keyboard.

![Preferences](img/users-form-profile-preferencias.webp)

Everything applies at once, only for you, and is saved in your account. Each group also has **“Like the site”**, which
follows whatever the administrator chose and says below what that is (for example, “Like the site: More contrast”). It
is what you have until you choose something else, and choosing it undoes your choice.

## The search box

In the top bar, **“Search features…”** (or **Ctrl K**) searches what you can open: the module screens, the
configuration, your account and, under **“Manual”**, the chapters and sections of this manual that apply to you. Type
part of the name; it ignores case and accents. If nothing matches, it says **“No results”**.

## Help

The question mark icon in the top bar opens the **help center**: this manual and, if your organization has it
configured, the form to **contact support**.

## The assistant

If the administrator has turned it on for you, next to the notifications bell you have the **assistant** button. On a
phone it is in the menu under your name. It opens a window where you ask in plain language about the platform: where
something is done (“Where do I create a new user?”), where this manual explains it (“Where is the manual for
publications?”) or what you have pending (“Do I have unread notifications?”).

- It answers **only with what you can see** on the platform, and below the answer it leaves buttons to go to the screens
  it names.
- **It only handles the platform.** If you ask it something else, it says it cannot and offers what it can do.
- **Enter** sends; **Shift+Enter** adds a line break. Each question, at most 1,000 characters.
- **The conversation is kept** even if you change screen or close the browser, on that same computer and only for you.
  It is erased on its own after a while without using the chat (one hour, unless the administrator sets another time),
  when you log out, and with **“New conversation”**, at the top of the window. **Esc** or the **×** close it without
  erasing it.
- If the assistant reached its usage limit, the window tells you so: when it renews or, if the limit does not renew on its
  own, that the administrator can reset it. If it is not available to you, the button does not appear.
