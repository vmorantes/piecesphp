# Directory

The Directory brings together the people and organizations whose public profile is visible.

## How to get there

From the side menu: **“Directory”**.

## What you see
<!-- ve: directory-admin-list -->

A table with three columns: **Actor** (the person or organization), **Location** and **Actions**.

![What you see](img/directory-admin-list.webp)

## What you can do

- **“View profile”**, in each row: opens the profile page of that person or organization. These are screens for reading; the breadcrumbs take you back to the Directory.
- **“Export current list”**: downloads what you are viewing.

!!! tip "Edit an organization"
    On an organization's profile page, the **“Edit”** button leads to its public profile. It is explained in the **Your organization** chapter.

## A person's profile
<!-- ve: profile-admin-profile -->

From the **Directory**, the **“View profile”** button of a person opens their public profile page. At the top, the breadcrumbs take you back to the Directory. It is a screen for reading: it has no fields to fill in.

![A person's profile](img/profile-admin-profile.webp)

## An organization's profile page
<!-- ve: profile-organization-admin-profile -->

**“View profile”** opens the organization's profile page, with the breadcrumbs **Home › Directory › organization name**. It is a screen for reading. The **“Edit”** button leads to its public profile.

![An organization's profile page](img/profile-organization-admin-profile.webp)
